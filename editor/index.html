WORLD EDITOR — HOW IT WORKS ⎙ printable cheat sheet✕ close (Esc)

Camera

Right-drag look around · Middle-drag pan · Wheel zoom · W A S D or the arrow keys fly, E up, Q down, hold Shift to fly fast (speed slider in the EDITOR fold) · F jump to the selected thing

Tab — free look for laptops: just move the finger on the trackpad to look around (no button held), WASD to fly. Tab, a click or Esc goes back to the normal cursor.

Placing shapes

Pick a shape on the left, then click the ground or any surface. R / Shift+R turn the preview 15°, , / . turn it 90°. Hold Shift while clicking to snap to a 0.5 m grid. Esc or C goes back to Select.

Editing a shape

Click it. 1 move · 2 rotate · 3 resize, then drag the coloured handles (hold Shift to snap). Resizing normally grows both sides from the middle; hold Alt while dragging and only the side the handle points at moves — the opposite face stays put. The panel on the right has exact numbers: size, corner radius, colour. PgUp / PgDn lift or lower it, End sits it on the ground. Ctrl+D copies it, Delete removes it, Ctrl+Z undoes. Shift+click selects several — dragging the move gizmo moves them all together.

Rooms (hollow)

Select a shape and tick Hollow — it becomes a room with walls, floor and roof. To make one building out of several shapes, Shift+click them all and press G. Push shapes into each other so their insides join. X hides all roofs so you can build inside. L shows name tags — give shapes a Name in the panel so your teammate can see what they're meant to be. Wall tool inside a room: press, drag along the floor, release — a floor-to-ceiling wall from A to B (Shift snaps it straight). Double-click anywhere to orbit around that point.

Doors and windows

Opening tool: press on a wall, drag a rectangle, let go — it cuts straight through. Low on the wall you get a door frame; higher up a plain window hole. A single click makes a standard door-sized opening. Openings can be moved and resized afterwards (the hole follows) and deleted (the wall heals). Door tool: click inside an opening to hang a door; the side you click is the hinge side.

Outside

Path and Fence: click points, double-click (or Enter) to finish, Esc to cancel. Tree brush: drag to scatter trees, [ / ] changes the brush size. Terrain: drag to raise / lower / smooth / flatten; RESET mode brings ground back to flat, and the panel has a button to reset all of it.

Play

P or ▶ PLAY starts at the spawn marker. W A S D walk, Shift run, F interact, L torch, hold right mouse to aim the torch, Esc back to the editor.

Saving

SAVE (Ctrl+S) saves the world online for the team under the name in the top bar — the first time it asks for your name and the room code. WORLDS lists everyone's worlds: LOAD one, or open its HISTORY to go back to an earlier version (every save keeps the old ones). The editor also autosaves to this browser every 30 s. EXPORT downloads a .json file; IMPORT loads one.
