Pick Box B, Cylinder, Sphere, Ramp or Wall, then click the ground or any surface — shapes stack on each other.

Click it. Then:

1 2 3
move / rotate / resize handles (hold Shift to snap)

PgUp PgDn

End
sit it on the ground, never sunk in
Ctrl+D

Delete

undo
Shift+click
select several at once

Rooms hollow

  1. Select a shape and tick Hollow — it gets walls, a floor and a roof you can walk into. Untick for the solid back.
  2. Several shapes into one building: Shift+click them, press G. Push them into each other so the insides join.
  3. X hides roofs. A Wall dropped on a room's floor sizes itself floor-to-ceiling.

Wall thickness is in the panel. Resizing a room with the handles resizes the whole room.

Doors & windows

Opening O
Press on a wall, drag a rectangle, let go — it cuts through. From the floor = door frame; higher up = window. A plain click = door-sized opening.
Afterwards
move or resize the opening and the hole follows; delete it and the wall heals.
Door
click inside an opening to hang a door. The half you click is the hinge side; it opens inward.
Door panel
locked + Key name → needs a pickup with that name

Outside

Fence N

Tree brush

Terrain T

Map size

Light

Saving & sharing

SAVE Ctrl+S
saves online for the team under the name in the top bar. First time: your name + the room code.
WORLDS
everyone's worlds — LOAD one, or HISTORY to go back to an earlier version. Nothing is ever overwritten.
EXPORT / IMPORT
download / load a .json file
Autosave
to this browser every 30 s — a local safety net only

Tips

  • Build rough first: boxes at roughly the right size, hollow them, cut openings, walk it. Detail comes later in Unreal.
  • Name your world in the top bar before the first SAVE.
  • If you can't select something, press X — the roof was probably in the way.
  • Everything is undoable with Ctrl+Z, including terrain and openings.
